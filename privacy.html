2. What the app processes without an account

Sawasdee works entirely without an account. Learning progress (learned words, review schedule, streak, settings, activity history) is stored exclusively locally on the device (app storage). If you have enabled your operating system's device backup (Android backup with Google, iCloud Backup), it may include the app storage; you control this in the system settings. There is no tracking, and there are no advertising SDKs or analytics services.

Audio playback uses recordings by native speakers that are included in the app or, as a fallback, the operating system's speech output; no data is transmitted to us in the process. The “Repeat after” exercises (microphone) and the placement test run entirely on the device: your recording is only cached temporarily for immediate playback, is not stored permanently and is not transmitted.

Audio packs. Recordings that are not included in the app (levels from A2 up, or target languages without bundled recordings) are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). You can download them in full under Settings → Vocabulary audio; without a download, the app fetches only the single recording from there the first time you play a word, and stores it for offline use. For technical reasons your device transmits its IP address and the address of the requested file (language and recording number) to GitHub. No other data is sent. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; transfer to the USA on the basis of the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (GitHub Privacy Statement).

3. Optional account and cloud synchronisation (Supabase)

If you sign in under “Account & Premium” – with a one-time code sent by email, with “Continue with Google” or (on iPhone) with “Continue with Apple” – the following is stored:

  • your email address and a sign-in timestamp; when signing in via Google, additionally the account identifier transmitted by Google, your name and, where applicable, the profile picture URL (Google LLC; Google's privacy policy applies). We request only the basic data (email, profile) from Google,
  • when signing in via “Continue with Apple”, additionally the pseudonymous account identifier transmitted by Apple and – only on the very first sign-in and only if you do not hide them – your name and your email address (if the “Hide My Email” feature is enabled, a relay address generated by Apple instead of your real email address); provider: Apple Inc., Apple's privacy policy applies,
  • your learning progress as records per target language (words/sentences with review status, streak, learned characters, activity history, your own word lists, confusion pairs, chosen session length, the first target language you chose).

Purpose: backing up and syncing progress between devices. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract).

Provider: Supabase Inc.; the data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt, eu-central-1) region. Supabase acts as our processor under Art. 28 GDPR; data processing agreement: https://supabase.com/legal/dpa.

The one-time code is sent by email via Brevo (Sendinblue GmbH, Cologne); in doing so, Brevo processes your email address and delivery metadata. https://www.brevo.com/de/legal/termsofuse/#dpa.

Deletion: Under Settings → Account & Premium → “Delete account” you delete your account and cloud data immediately and permanently; the local progress on the device is retained. In doing so, we remove the email address as well as device and diagnostic data from feedback reports you sent while signed in; the report text is retained for up to 12 months (section 4). The identifier held by the purchase status service RevenueCat (section 5) is retained as proof of your purchases. Without deletion, we store the data for as long as the account exists.

4. Feedback function

Via “Report a problem” you voluntarily send us a message. The following is sent along with it: app version, platform (Android/iOS/Web), browser/device identifier (user agent), a random identifier generated per installation (so that you can see in the app which of your reports have been accepted), selected languages, where applicable the reported word, where applicable the error message of the last app crash and – optionally – your email address for follow-up questions. If you switch on “Attach diagnostics” when sending (off by default), the report additionally contains up to the last 50 usage events of this installation (e.g. “session started”, “paywall opened”, error code – without texts, recordings or exact timestamps) and your 7-day recall rate; you see this data in a preview before sending. Without this option, it is not transmitted; the log never leaves your device in the background. After a learning session that was ended early, the app asks for the reason at most once a day; the answer is only sent if you submit the report. Storage with Supabase (EU); a notification is sent to us via Brevo. If you have provided an email address, we send you a short message via Brevo as soon as we have accepted your report or completed our review of it. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (bug fixing/product improvement). Reports are deleted automatically 12 months after receipt.

5. Purchases / Premium

Premium (monthly or yearly subscription, one-time purchase) is purchased, depending on the device, via Google Play or, on iPhone, via the App Store; payment processing is handled accordingly by Google (Google Play Terms of Service and Google's privacy policy) or Apple (Apple's privacy policy); we do not receive any payment data. To check the purchase status (“Premium yes/no”, term, trial period) we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., USA); what is transmitted is an anonymous app user ID generated per installation (if you are signed in, the pseudonymous identifier of your account instead, so that purchases apply on all your devices) as well as the purchase receipts and purchase history of the respective store (Google Play or App Store) – no email address or name is transmitted. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Transfer to the USA on the basis of Standard Contractual Clauses / EU-US Data Privacy Framework. https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

5a. Invite friends (optional, account only)

Signed-in users receive a personal invite code under Settings → Account & Premium. If you redeem a code, we store: the identifiers of both accounts and the time of redemption, whether the invited person subsequently learned on three different days (determined from the activity history that is synchronised anyway, see section 3), whether they purchased Premium (query of the purchase status at RevenueCat using the account identifier, see section 5) and the Premium days credited as a result. To unlock these days, we transmit the account identifier and the expiry date to RevenueCat. The inviting person only sees counts (invited / learning / Premium), never names, email addresses or learning content of the invitees.

Purpose: running the invitation programme. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. The data is deleted together with the respective account; days already credited to the other person remain. An exception is an irreversibly computed check value (SHA-256 hash) of your email address together with the time of redemption: it is retained even after the account has been deleted and serves exclusively to prevent an invitation from being redeemed more than once with the same email address (legal basis: legitimate interest in preventing abuse, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). It is deleted as soon as the invitation programme ends.
